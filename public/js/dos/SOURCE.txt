js-dos 8.3.0 and the emulators 8.2.1 it loads
=============================================

The files in this folder are js-dos, a DOSBox frontend for the browser, and
the emulators package it loads. They are redistributed unmodified under the
GNU General Public License, version 2 (see COPYING in this folder).

  js-dos       https://github.com/caiiiycuk/js-dos        (tag v8.3.0)
  emulators    https://github.com/caiiiycuk/emulators     (version 8.2.1)

The vendored files are:

  js-dos.js
  js-dos.css
  emulators/emulators.js
  emulators/wdosbox.js
  emulators/wdosbox.wasm
  emulators/wlibzip.js
  emulators/wlibzip.wasm

The complete corresponding source is the upstream release named above. It is
also reachable from this repository's third-party notices at
docs/THIRD-PARTY.md, which carries the same source offer.
